3D Portfolio

Immersive AI-Driven 3D Portfolio

3D Portfolio is a cutting-edge, AI-driven portfolio experience that combines immersive 3D visuals with a conversational AI assistant named Guava. It showcases projects, skills, and expertise in a way that traditional portfolios cannot.

Powered by Three.js for 3D rendering and Puter.js for AI integration, this portfolio is built with a "Vanilla-First" architecture—lightweight, fast, and bundle-free.

Under the visionary leadership of Sulaiman Sa'eed Sulaiman, this portfolio pushes the boundaries of what a personal brand can achieve on the web.
